# Ethics in the age of Generative AI

Good Uses:
- Farmers can use generative AI to predict the weather and utilize it to plan their seeding and harvests.
- Using good data to help HR in organizations to minimize conflicts and prmote fair promotions.

Bad Uses:
- Deep Fakes creating false natative to swing election or prospective of the masses.
- Inaccurate Chatbots specially in the medical field where wrong information can have huge implecation of life of people.

# Ethical AI Framework

**Any new technology is only as ethical as the underlying data that it't trained on**

- Responsible data practises
    - What is the source of the training data?
    - What has been done to reduce bias in the data?
    - How might the data we're using prepetuate historic bias?
    - What opportunities exist to prevent biased decision making?
- Boundaries on safe and appropriate use
    - Who is the traget population for this tool?
    - What are he main goals and incentives?
    - What is the most responsible way to achieve these goals?
- Robust transparency
    - How did the tool arrive at its output?
    - What other ways do we have of testing fairness?
    - Can decision makers easily understand the input-analysis-output process?
    - Have you engaged with a broad range of stakeholders?
