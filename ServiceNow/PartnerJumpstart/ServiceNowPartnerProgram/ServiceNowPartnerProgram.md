# Inside the ServiceNow Partner Program

## Partner performance and progressions is defined by the 3 C's

- Competency : Building skills and certifications the team needs.
- Capability
- Customer Success : 
    - Customer success is defined by % of deployed applications with a certified lead application consultant.
    - \# of approved customer success stories.

## Different Modules in Sevice Now Partners:

[Service Now Partners](https://www.servicenow.com/partners.html)
- Reseller: 
    - Requires credentidaled resources armed with knowledge about positioning and selling Service Now.
- Consulting & Implementation:
    - Requires credentials resources knowledgeable about selling ServiceNow
    - Resources with core platform certification.
    - Certified Implementation sepcialists.
    - Delivery Accredentions.
- Service Prooviders:
    - Requires credentialed resources knowledgable about selling ServiceNow.
    - Resources with technical expertise to provide managd service offerings.
- Build:
    - Partners need knowledge on how to develop and pubilish integrations, applications, solutions and AI Agents on the ServiceNow Store.

## Different level of Partner Tier:

- Registered
    - Partner Portal
    - Recognition through product line achievements
    - Basic program tools
- Select
    - Visibility on partner finder
    - access to incentive programs and awards
    - Market development fund
- Premier
    - Co-marketing funds.
    - Service Provider built on servicenow eligibility
    - Deeper engagement with serviceNow teams
- Elite
    - Partner Manager Support
    - Roadmap reviews
    - Exclusive events & sponsorship opportunities.
- Global Elite
    - Top-tier visibility
    - Priority access to serviceNow resources.
    - Strategic Influence across the ecosystem.
