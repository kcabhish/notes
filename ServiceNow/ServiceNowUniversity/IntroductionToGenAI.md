# What is generative AI

Generative AI is a branch of artificial intelligence that employs deep-learning models to create human-language text, images, and other content by learning patterns from existing data. Generative AI supports human interactions in context, getting and providing help, resolving issues, and offloading routine tasks.

# Real World Applications Of Generative AI

- Healthcare
    - Generative AI is being used to identify anomalies and create personalized reports that are submitted to radiologists for review. Instead of displacing them, AI has drastically improved their efficiency while maintaining accuracy.
- Education
    - People often think about the risks of students using generative AI in school. When used properly, gen AI has been shown to increase student creativity, build, and explore topics they normally wouldn't be able to. It has also been used by educators to create learning materials and assessments.
- Finance
    - CFOs and finance departments are increasingly relying on gen AI to create reports that allow them to make decisions on resource allocations and updates to their financial models.
- Energy
    - Generative AI has been cited as an important tool to modernize energy grids and streamline permitting.
- Application Developmet
    - Coding with generative AI has dominated the conversation in the past few years, and adoption has become mandatory for work. Major tech companies have recently reported that anywhere from 30 to 75 percent of their code is AI-generated.

## Limitations And Risks

- Generative AI models may struggle to tune their outputs to new circumstances, requiring ongoing fine-tuning and adjustment to ensure relevance and accuracy. 
- Generative AI systems may generate inaccurate or unproven answers. Generative AI ‘hallucinations,’ where the AI may simply make up answers, represent major issues, particularly in scenarios that demand complete accuracy and validity. It is crucial to carefully assess the outputs for accuracy, appropriateness, and actual usefulness before relying on or publicly distributing any information produced by a generative AI.
- The recent focus on ease of use and conversation-like input/output has created an environment where it can be difficult to really understand how the generative AI works and where its original data comes from. Many organizations may not wish to take full advantage of generative AI without a clearer picture of its inner workings.

### Additional Risks associated with Generative AI

- Harmful Bias
    - Because generative AI models are built on a foundation of data, they may carry certain biases from that training data across into their outputs. Organizations must have policies or controls in place to detect and address biased content so as not to inadvertently spread harmful preconceptions.
- Intellectual Property Rights
    - Today's generative AIs, while advanced, still depend on existing data for training, which raises copyright concerns. These systems may use intellectual property that doesn't belong to their developers. Users sharing confidential information with AIs might find their data is no longer private and may become part of the AI's dataset.
- New Cybersecurity Dangers
    - Cybercriminals are using generative AI to target victims and bypass security measures. They exploit vulnerabilities in company-based AIs to access sensitive data. Staying updated on cybersecurity advancements and applying patches is crucial to counter these threats.
- Fake, misusem and misinformation
    - A major concern for regulators, governments, and the public is the use of generative AI to create false, inappropriate content. Generative AI is also used by bad actors to spread misinformation. Regulations like the 2024 EU AI Act are being passed to govern AI use and protect the public.
- Environmental Costs
    - Massive data centers are AI's physical footprint in the real world. These data centers consume tremendous amounts of resources (water and electricity). Communities around the world are considering the environmental costs of these systems.
