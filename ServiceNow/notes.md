# Center of Excellence and Innovation

# Terms

- Workspaces
    A workspace is a suite of tools displayed in a single-pane view that help resolve customer problems and provide answers to questions that may have.

- Lists
    The list view displays records from a table in the platform. 

- Forms
    A form displayes fields from one record where users can view and edit the record data.

- Dashboard 
    A dashboard is a custom arrangement of widgets and enables you to display multople performance analytics and reporting on a single screen.

- Knowledge Articles
    Knowledge articles are uploaded to the platform in specific categories to help platform users receive information or help about heir job role or function.

- Task
     A task is any record that can be assigned to or completed by a user in the Platform. They are updated as the work defined by the task is completed (moved to a Closed State).

- VTBs (Visual Task Boards)
    Transform your lists and form into an interactive graphical experience. Similar to JIRA.
